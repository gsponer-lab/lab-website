> about gsponerlab

Computational biology · University of British Columbia

Protein design, computational biology, protein biophysics, bioinformatics

The Gsponer Lab uses gen-AI, machine learning, computational biology and statistical analysis to analyse protein structure and function across a range of landscapes. The lab's main projects are designing protein binders that bind specific targets, building affinity and interaction prediction methods, and researching the role of phosphorylation as a driver of protein–protein interactions and protein switches. The lab has a specific focus on intrinsically disordered protein structures.
