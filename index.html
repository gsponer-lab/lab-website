> about gsponerlab

Computational biology · University of British Columbia

Protein design, computational biology, protein biophysics, bioinformatics

The Gsponer Lab is a leader in the study of protein interactions and protein-based molecular systems, with a particular focus on intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) and intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs), the “dark matter” of the proteome whose lack of a unique tertiary structure allows them to act as molecular Swiss army knives, regulating diverse cellular processes through highly versatile interactions. The lab investigates both intermolecular interactions, including how IDR-mediated contacts between proteins drive signalling and disease, and intramolecular dynamics such as autoinhibition and conformational switching. To this end, the lab develops and applies a suite of computational tools, combining biophysical principles, machine learning, and generative AI, to characterise dynamic protein systems at scale, extract design rules, and engineer novel binders targeting IDR-mediated interaction interfaces.
